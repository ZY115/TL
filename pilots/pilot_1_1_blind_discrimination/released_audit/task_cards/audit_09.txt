Satisfy at least one complete alternative. The first alternative requires visits to A, then B, then C at strictly increasing indices and also requires never entering X. The second alternative requires at least one D, requires every D to have a B at an offset from 1 through 2, inclusive, and requires avoiding X globally until A is reached; that until clause requires a current or future A, forbids X strictly before its selected A, and allows X on the A endpoint. The D response rule is vacuously true without D, but the second alternative separately requires D. Satisfying both alternatives is allowed. Clauses may not be mixed across alternatives: one entire alternative must succeed.
