Meet all four clauses: visit A before a strictly later B; after every A visit C at an offset from 2 through 4, inclusive; after every B visit D at an offset from 1 through 2, inclusive; and avoid X globally until C is reached. The global until clause requires a current or future C, forbids X strictly before its selected C, and allows X on that C endpoint. Each trigger rule is vacuously true without its trigger, although the strict-order clause requires A and B.
