Visit A, then B, then C at strictly increasing step indices. Also, after every A visit D at an offset from 1 through 3, inclusive. After every B, avoid X until D is reached: for each B a D at that same step or a later step is required, X is forbidden strictly between that B and its selected D, and X is allowed on the D endpoint. Each trigger rule is vacuously true without its trigger, although the strict-order clause requires at least one A and B.
